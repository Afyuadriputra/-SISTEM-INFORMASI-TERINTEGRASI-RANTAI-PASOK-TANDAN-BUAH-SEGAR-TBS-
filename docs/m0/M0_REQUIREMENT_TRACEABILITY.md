## 3. Matriks Kepatuhan Non-Fungsional (`PRD NFR-001`..`NFR-020`), `BIZ-001`..`BIZ-030`, & Ruang Lingkup Ditangguhkan

- **Non-Fungsional (`NFR-001`..`NFR-020`):**
  - **Ketersediaan, Kinerja & Skalabilitas (`NFR-001`, `NFR-002`, `NFR-003`):** Kontrak Redis cache-aside TTL (`public:prices:v1:*` & `public:mills:v1:*`), paginasi wajib (`PaginationMeta` max 100), pemisahan health probe `/health/live` & `/health/ready`, serta kontrak `SYS_REQUEST_TIMEOUT` (`504`) dan `SYS_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE` (`503`). (`COVERED`, verifikasi beban di `B11`).
  - **Keamanan, Sesi, CSRF & Privasi (`NFR-004`, `NFR-005`, `NFR-006`, `NFR-007`, `NFR-008`, `NFR-009`, `NFR-010`):** Kontrak session cookie `HttpOnly`, header `X-CSRF-Token`, fail-closed `401/403`, tidak mengekspos `password_hash`/PII di respons/error, pemisahan domain publik vs privat, serta `non_approver_admin` (`BR-SYS-002`). (`COVERED` via `K5, K6`).
  - **Integritas Data, Presisi & Auditabilitas (`NFR-011`, `NFR-012`, `NFR-013`, `NFR-014`, `NFR-015`, `NFR-016`):** String desimal berpola regex ketat (`DecimalKgString`, `DecimalMoneyString`, `DecimalPriceString`, `DecimalPercentString`), UUID v4, ISO-8601 UTC `Z`, **`Idempotency-Key` wajib pada 8 operasi mutasi kritis** (mewakili 7 kategori proses kritis `BSD §27.2`: `createReceiptDraft`, `reserveContractQuota`, `issueDeliveryOrder`, `createMillTicket`, `createOrTriggerReconciliation`, `calculateReconciliation`, `approveOrRejectInvoicePayment`, `recordPaymentEvidence`), optimistic locking `version`, `X-Request-ID`, serta `listAuditEvents`. (`COVERED` via `K2, K6, K9`).
  - **Usability, Aksesibilitas & Pemeliharaan (`NFR-017`, `NFR-018`, `NFR-019`, `NFR-020`):** Penyelarasan dengan D5 FigCraft (`WCAG` 0 gagal kontras, 7 layar mobile kritis, pesan error aman Bahasa Indonesia `error-catalog.yaml`) serta arsitektur modular inkremental + ADR-001..010. (`COVERED`).
- **Skenario Bisnis (`BIZ-001`..`BIZ-030`):**
  - Terpetakan ke aturan OpenAPI, `state-catalog.yaml` (`SM-01`..`SM-11`), `error-catalog.yaml`, dan `contracts/fixtures/*.json`: `BIZ-001`, `BIZ-002`, `BIZ-003` (`public-prices.json`, `SM-01`); `BIZ-004`, `BIZ-005`, `BIZ-006` (`tbs-receipts.json`, `SM-02`, `NEG-06`); `BIZ-007`, `BIZ-008`, `BIZ-009` (`contracts-quota.json`, `SM-03`, `SM-04`, `NEG-08`); `BIZ-010`, `BIZ-030` (`delivery-orders.json`, `SM-05`, `NEG-07`); `BIZ-011`, `BIZ-012` (`mill-tickets.json`, `SM-06`, `NEG-03`, `NEG-10`); `BIZ-013`, `BIZ-014`, `BIZ-015`, `BIZ-016`, `BIZ-017`, `BIZ-029` (`reconciliations.json`, `SM-07`, `SM-08`, `NEG-11`, `NEG-21`); `BIZ-018`, `BIZ-019`, `BIZ-020`, `BIZ-021`, `BIZ-022`, `BIZ-026`, `BIZ-027` (`invoices-payments.json`, `SM-09`..`SM-11`, `NEG-12..14`); `BIZ-023`, `BIZ-024`, `BIZ-025`, `BIZ-028` (`actors.json`, `role-permissions.yaml`, `NEG-01..04`, `NEG-15..19`).
- **Fitur Ditangguhkan / Non-API (`DEFERRED` / Out of Scope MVP per `PRD §23`):**
